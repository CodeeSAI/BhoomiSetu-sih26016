# 🌍 Overview

**BhoomiSetu** is a unified digital platform for **land acquisition management and governance**.

The platform brings together land records, geospatial information, acquisition projects, statutory workflows, compensation, rehabilitation and resettlement, affected families, analytics and administrative governance into one connected operational environment.

Land acquisition is not simply about acquiring a parcel of land.

It involves a chain of interconnected activities:

```text
Land
  ↓
Survey & Verification
  ↓
Project Planning
  ↓
Statutory Approvals
  ↓
Notifications
  ↓
Objection / Hearing
  ↓
Final Declaration
  ↓
Award
  ↓
Compensation
  ↓
Rehabilitation & Resettlement
  ↓
Possession
  ↓
Monitoring & Reporting

BhoomiSetu connects this lifecycle through a centralized interface designed for visibility, accountability and decision support.
🎯 Problem Statement
SIH 2026 — SIH26016
Land Acquisition Management
Land acquisition involves multiple stakeholders, departments, statutory stages, documents, financial transactions and affected families.
When these processes are distributed across disconnected systems, authorities may face difficulty in:
- Monitoring project progress
- Tracking acquisition milestones
- Identifying delayed projects
- Managing land parcels
- Coordinating approvals
- Tracking compensation
- Monitoring rehabilitation
- Maintaining affected-family information
- Generating consolidated reports
- Identifying workflow bottlenecks
Our approach
BhoomiSetu creates a unified operational layer connecting:
LAND + PROJECTS + GIS + WORKFLOW + PEOPLE + FINANCE + R&R + ANALYTICS
```
